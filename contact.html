Before you call

Helpful to have ready

You do not need everything — but these make your first call faster.

  • The full name of the person who needs help
  • The hospital, school, or barangay involved
  • Your latest bill, quotation, or assessment (if you have one)
  • The bank or e-wallet account where funds should go

See the full document checklist →

Emailing us?

Include these details

So we can help you in one reply.

  • Subject: “New fundraiser”, “Callback request”, “Donation”, or “Donors Circle”
  • Your mobile or landline and the best time to call
  • A short summary of who needs help and why
  • Photos of documents (optional — you can send them later)
Life-threatening emergency? Call 911 or go to the nearest hospital first. We are a fundraising service, not an emergency responder.
