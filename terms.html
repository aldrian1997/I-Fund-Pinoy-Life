Terms of Service

The terms that apply when families start a fundraiser with us and when donors give through I Fund Pinoy Life.

1. Who we are

I Fund Pinoy Life is an independent volunteer initiative that helps Filipino families raise funds for personal causes. We are not a government agency and are not connected to DSWD, PCSO, or PhilHealth. See our Volunteer Disclosure.

2. For families starting a fundraiser

  • You agree to give truthful information and genuine documents.
  • You confirm you are the beneficiary, their guardian, or have their consent.
  • You approve your fundraiser story before it is shared.
  • Funds must be used for the purpose described in your campaign.
  • We may decline, pause, or close a campaign that we cannot verify or that breaks these terms.

3. For donors

  • Donations are voluntary gifts to the family's cause and are generally non-refundable once disbursed.
  • If you believe a payment was made in error, contact us as soon as possible and we will do our best to help.
  • Donations are not tax-deductible unless we tell you otherwise in writing.

4. Collection and disbursement

We collect donations on behalf of the beneficiary, reconcile payments, and remit funds to the verified bank account or e-wallet on request.

5. Fees

There are no upfront fees. On withdrawal, 10% is deducted for volunteer allowances and operations and 3% for payment gateway processing. See the Fee Breakdown.

6. Verification limits

We verify every campaign carefully, but we cannot guarantee the outcome of any medical treatment, rebuilding effort, or education. Donors give based on the information presented.

7. Misuse and fraud

Providing false information or misusing funds may lead to the campaign being closed, remaining funds being held, and the matter being reported to authorities. Please report suspected misuse to info@ifundpinoy.life.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of our services means you accept the latest version.

Questions? Call 02 8351 5854 or email info@ifundpinoy.life.
