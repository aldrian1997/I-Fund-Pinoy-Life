Why there are no public campaign listings here

We keep every verified cause private to protect the medical privacy, safety, and dignity of each Filipino family. Only the people running a campaign can share its link.

Join the Weekly Donors List Donate by Campaign Code
  • Private encrypted link — families share it directly with relatives, friends, and church community.
  • No search listing — neighbours, debt collectors, and scammers cannot browse vulnerable families.
  • Weekly verified digest — donors without a link can receive vetted urgent cases, shared only with family consent.
  • You stay in control — you decide who sees your story and when it closes.

Why choose us

We do the hard work so you do not have to.

Starting a fundraiser when you are already going through hardship is overwhelming. That is why we handle everything from start to finish.

Personal, Human Service

You talk to a real person on the phone. We listen to your story and help you share it in a way that connects with donors.

Thorough Verification

Every campaign is checked before it goes live. This builds trust with donors and keeps the platform honest and reliable.

PayPal & Philippine Payment Rails

Donations come in through PayPal, GCash, Maya, and direct bank transfer. We collect, process, and remit the money to you.

Ongoing Support

We post updates on your behalf, answer donor questions, and keep the campaign moving from the first day to the last.

Payments

Every way your loved ones give — covered.

From a GCash transfer in Manila to a PayPal gift from an OFW in Dubai, we collect it, reconcile it, and send it to you.

E-wallets & banking

GCash, Maya, and direct bank transfer to BDO, BPI, Landbank, Metrobank, and InstaPay-enabled banks.

PayPal & cards

A secure global gateway for overseas Filipino workers and friends abroad. Visa and Mastercard accepted.

Transparent 10% + 3%

No upfront cost. On withdrawal, 10% supports volunteer operations and 3% covers payment gateway fees. See the breakdown.
