Guide for generous donors & OFWs

How to give and support Filipino families in need

We put family privacy first. There is no public directory — so here is how to give with confidence through secure private links and our verified weekly donor digest.

Privacy-first policy

Unlisted private links

We protect families from public exposure, debt harassers, and social stigma. Here is how you can still find and support verified causes.

1. You received a link

If a friend, relative, co-worker, or church member sent you an official ifundpinoy.life/c/… link, that is their private verified fundraiser. You can donate directly on their page.

Only have a campaign code? Enter it on our donation page.

2. You do not have a link

Join our Verified Donors Circle. Each week, with direct family consent, we email a private digest to generous donors featuring verified urgent medical and calamity cases.

Or give to our Emergency Pool for urgent dialysis, ICU, and calamity cases.

Verified Donors Circle

Get the confidential weekly digest

Receive one email a week featuring vetted Filipino families currently facing urgent medical bills and emergencies. To join, simply send us an email — we will add you personally.

Where your money goes

How your donation flows

1

You give

Send your donation via PayPal, GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or debit/credit card.

2

We collect & receipt

We securely collect the money on behalf of the family, reconcile payments, and issue an acknowledgement receipt.

3

We disburse

When the family requests a withdrawal, we remit the funds directly to their verified bank account or e-wallet.

4

Fully transparent

For every disbursement, 10% covers volunteer operations and 3% covers third-party payment gateway fees.

Giving from abroad

For OFWs and Filipinos overseas

Wherever you are — Dubai, Riyadh, Hong Kong, Singapore, Toronto, or California — you can still be there for your kababayan back home.

  • PayPal and international cards work from most countries. Currency conversion is handled automatically.
  • Remittance-friendly. Prefer sending through your usual remittance to a PH bank? Email us for bank details.
  • Different time zone? Email donate@ifundpinoy.life anytime — we reply within one business day.

Give safely

How to make sure a fundraiser is genuine

Scammers sometimes copy the look of real fundraisers. A few simple checks keep your kindness going to the right family.

Check the link

Official campaign pages live on ifundpinoy.life. Be careful with look-alike spellings or shortened links from strangers.

Call to confirm

Unsure about a campaign or a payment request? Call 02 8351 5854 or email info@ifundpinoy.life before you give.

Report anything odd

If someone uses our name to ask for money in a way that feels wrong, tell us right away. We take every report seriously.

Questions before you give?

Our volunteers are happy to help. Call 02 8351 5854 or email donate@ifundpinoy.life and we will answer within one business day.
