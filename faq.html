For families

How do I start a fundraiser?

Just call 02 8351 5854, Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. A volunteer will ask about your situation and guide you. If you cannot call, email info@ifundpinoy.life with your number and we will call you back.

Do I need a smartphone, Facebook, or tech skills?

No. That is exactly why we exist. We write your story, build your page, and handle payments. All you need is a phone to talk to us and a way to send photos of your documents (a relative's phone is fine).

How long before my page is ready?

Usually within 24 hours after we finish verifying your documents. Urgent hospital cases are prioritised.

Can I start a fundraiser for someone else?

Yes. Relatives, friends, and church members often call on behalf of a family. We just need the beneficiary's (or their guardian's) consent before the page goes live, and funds are remitted to the beneficiary or their verified representative.

What documents do I need?

It depends on the cause. For medical cases, usually a hospital Statement of Account, a medical abstract, a barangay certificate of indigency, and a valid ID.

See the full checklist on our How It Works page. Don't worry if you are missing some — call us and we will guide you.

Is there a minimum or maximum goal?

No fixed minimum or maximum. We help you set a realistic goal based on your bills or quotations, so donors can see exactly what their gift supports.

Privacy & safety

Why are there no public campaign listings on your website?

We intentionally protect the dignity, medical privacy, and personal safety of our families. We do NOT publish a public directory of vulnerable people. Each family receives their own private, unlisted link. Only the people who run the campaign can share it with friends, family, church, colleagues, and our verified Donors Circle.

Who can see my fundraiser?

Only people who have your private link, plus — only if you agree — donors in our weekly Verified Donors Circle digest. Your page is not listed or searchable on our website.

How do you handle my medical records?

Documents are used only to verify your campaign. We never publish them without your permission. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How can I tell if a fundraiser using your name is real?

Official pages live on ifundpinoy.life. If you are unsure, call 02 8351 5854 or email info@ifundpinoy.life before giving.

Donations & payments

How do donations get collected and given to the family?

Donors give via PayPal, GCash, Maya, bank transfer, or card. We collect the money on behalf of the family, reconcile it, and issue receipts. When the family requests a withdrawal, we remit the funds directly to their verified bank account or e-wallet.

Can Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) donate?

Yes. PayPal and international Visa/Mastercard work from most countries and handle currency conversion automatically. You can also send to a Philippine bank account — email us for details.

How can someone who wants to help find causes in need?

Join our Verified Donors Circle by emailing us. Each week we send a private digest of verified urgent cases, shared only with family consent. You can also give to our Emergency Pool.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. After we confirm your payment, we send an acknowledgement receipt to your email.

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes. Just tell us when you email or call, and your name will not be shown to the public or on the family's page.

Fees & withdrawals

What are your fees for withdrawing funds?

There are zero upfront fees. On withdrawal, 10% covers volunteer team allowances and operations, and 3% covers payment gateway processing.

Try the fee calculator to see what your family receives.

When can we withdraw?

You can request a withdrawal anytime during the campaign — for example, when a hospital payment is due. Many families withdraw in parts as bills come in.

Where do the funds go?

To a verified bank account (BDO, BPI, Landbank, Metrobank, and others) or e-wallet (GCash, Maya) in the name of the beneficiary or their verified representative.

What if we raise more than our goal?

Extra funds stay with the family's campaign and can be used for related costs such as follow-up medicines and recovery.

About us

Are you a government office or DSWD?

No. We are an independent group of Filipino volunteers. We are not connected to DSWD, PCSO, PhilHealth, or any government agency.

Why should I trust you?

Every campaign is verified with documents before it goes live, fees are published openly, and you can always reach a real person by phone or email. We would rather say no to a campaign than risk a donor's trust.

Can I volunteer with you?

We would love to hear from you. Email info@ifundpinoy.life with a short introduction and how you would like to help.

Still have a question?
