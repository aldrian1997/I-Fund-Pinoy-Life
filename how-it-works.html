How I Fund Pinoy Life works

You do not need to know tech. You just need to call us. We take care of everything from your story to your donation page — and we stay with you until you reach your goal.

Call Us: 02 8351 5854Email for a Callback

Step-by-step

From your phone call to receiving funds

We removed every technical obstacle so you can focus entirely on your family.

1
About 10 minutes on the phone

Call Us

Ring 02 8351 5854. Tell us about your situation — who needs help, why, and how much you need to raise. No forms, apps, or tech skills required.

  • Talk to a compassionate, friendly volunteer intake coordinator.
  • We help you estimate hospital or emergency needs with dignity.
  • Can't call right now? Email us your number and we will call you back.
2
100% trust checked

We Verify

Our team reviews your cause and confirms your identity. We may ask for documents like hospital bills or barangay certificates to protect donors and build trust.

  • Snap photos of your documents and send them via Messenger or email.
  • We review hospital records so donors can give with total confidence.
  • Missing a paper? We will guide you on what to request and from where.
3
Private unlisted page

We Build Your Page

We write your fundraiser story with you, set up your private donation page, and connect multi-channel payments. Your page is usually ready within 24 hours of verification.

  • Privacy first: only you receive the link. You control who sees it.
  • We connect PayPal, GCash, Maya, direct bank transfer, and cards.
  • You review and approve your story before anything goes live.
4
We collect & give to you

You Collect

We collect donations through PayPal, bank transfer, GCash, and Maya. We reconcile the funds, then remit them directly to your bank account or e-wallet.

  • Direct payout to your BDO, BPI, Landbank, GCash, or Maya account.
  • Clear, transparent deduction: 10% volunteer operations + 3% gateway.
  • We post updates and answer donor questions until you reach your goal.

Your first call

What we will ask you on the phone

There are no wrong answers. If you do not know something yet, that is okay — we will figure it out together.

Calling for someone else? That is fine. A relative, friend, or church member can start the call. We will just need the beneficiary's consent before the page goes live.

Who needs help

The patient or beneficiary, and your relationship to them.

What happened

A short account in your own words — Tagalog, Bisaya, English, or Taglish is fine.

How much is needed

Your best estimate. We will help you work it out from bills or quotations.

How urgent it is

Hospital deadlines, discharge dates, or school enrolment cut-offs.

Where to send funds

The bank account or e-wallet in your name where we will remit.

Who you will share with

Family, friends, church, or co-workers — so we can tailor your story.

Verification guide

Simple documents to prepare

Don't worry if you don't have all of these right now. Call us first, and our team will guide you on what to request from your hospital, school, or barangay.

Hospital Statement of Account (SOA)

Recent running hospital bill or pharmacy quotation.

Medical Abstract / Doctor's Prescription

Signed by the attending physician or surgeon.

Barangay Certificate of Indigency

Validates local residency and hardship.

Valid Government ID

Of the patient, parent, or primary caregiver.

Photos of the Damage

Clear pictures of your home or property after the event.

Barangay Certification

Confirming you are an affected resident of the area.

Fire or Incident Report

From the BFP or local authority, if available.

Valid Government ID

Of the head of household or primary contact.

Assessment / Statement of Fees

From the school registrar or accounting office.

Certificate of Enrolment or Admission

Or a letter of acceptance for incoming students.

Latest Report Card / Transcript

Helps donors see the student's commitment.

Valid ID of Student and Parent

School ID plus a government ID of the guardian.

Proof of the Emergency

Death certificate, funeral quotation, termination notice, or similar.

Barangay Certificate of Indigency

Validates local residency and hardship.

Quotation or Billing

What the funds will pay for, from the provider.

Valid Government ID

Of the beneficiary or primary caregiver.

Our transparent fee policy: 10% + 3%

We do not have corporate backers or government subsidies. We still need to pay for phone lines, hosting, and modest allowances for the volunteers who speak with families all day.
