Medical & Hospital Bills

Hospital bills can grow faster than any family can keep up with. We help you raise funds so your loved one can be treated — and come home.

Volunteer tip: Ask the hospital billing section for an updated Statement of Account. Donors respond best when they see a clear, current figure.

What we can help raise funds for

  • Dialysis sessions and kidney care
  • Surgery, operations, and ICU confinement
  • Cancer treatment and chemotherapy
  • Dengue, pneumonia, and emergency admissions
  • Maintenance medicines for the elderly
  • Therapy and rehabilitation

Calamity Relief & Rebuilding

When typhoons, floods, fires, or earthquakes take everything, we help families get back on their feet with the support of their own community.

Volunteer tip: Take photos of the damage as soon as it is safe. A barangay certification confirming you are affected helps a lot.

What we can help raise funds for

  • Roofing sheets, lumber, and home repairs
  • Food, water, and hygiene essentials
  • Replacing lost school supplies and uniforms
  • Temporary shelter or rent
  • Replacing tools needed for livelihood
  • Fire victims and house rebuilding

Education & Tuition

A diploma can change a family's future. We help students and parents raise funds for school without the embarrassment of a public appeal.

Volunteer tip: Your school's assessment form and the student's latest grades help donors see exactly what their gift supports.

What we can help raise funds for

  • College and senior high school tuition
  • Miscellaneous school and laboratory fees
  • Board exam review and licensure fees
  • Support for orphaned or working students
  • Laptops and gadgets required for study
  • Dormitory or boarding costs

Personal Hardship & Emergencies

Some emergencies do not fit in a box. If your family is facing a sudden crisis, call us and we will see how we can help.

Volunteer tip: Every situation is different. Call us first and we will tell you honestly whether and how we can help.

What we can help raise funds for

  • Funeral, wake, and burial expenses
  • Sudden job loss or loss of a breadwinner
  • Bringing home a stranded or sick OFW
  • Persons with disability needing assistive devices
  • Elderly care without family support
  • Other urgent, verifiable needs

Our standards

What we cannot accept

To keep every campaign honest and protect donors, we do not build fundraisers for:

  • Causes we are unable to verify with documents or a reference
  • Campaigns for someone without their consent (or their guardian's)
  • Paying off lending, gambling, or online loan debts
  • Business capital or investment schemes
  • Political campaigns or partisan activities
  • Anything illegal, misleading, or harmful

Not sure if your situation qualifies? Call 02 8351 5854 or email info@ifundpinoy.life. We will always give you an honest answer.
