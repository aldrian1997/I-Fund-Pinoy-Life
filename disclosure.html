Volunteer Disclosure

1. We are not a government agency

I Fund Pinoy Life is not the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD), the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO), PhilHealth, a local government unit, or any other government office. We are not connected to, endorsed by, or funded by them.

If you are seeking government medical or financial assistance, please apply directly with those offices. Applying with us does not affect your eligibility there.

2. We are a volunteer initiative

We are a group of everyday Filipino volunteers who help families that lack the technology, skills, or time to run a fundraiser on their own. Our volunteers answer calls, verify documents, write stories, and handle disbursements.

3. How we are funded

We have no corporate backers or government subsidies. Our operations are funded by the 10% operations fee deducted on withdrawal and by direct gifts to support our volunteers. A separate 3% covers third-party payment gateway fees. See the fee breakdown.

4. How we handle money

Donations are collected on behalf of each family, reconciled, receipted, and remitted to the family's verified bank account or e-wallet when they request a withdrawal. Every remittance comes with a written breakdown of fees.
