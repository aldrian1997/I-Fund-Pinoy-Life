Our story & purpose

We do the hard work so you do not have to.

I Fund Pinoy Life was founded by Filipinos who saw that families without smartphones, tech skills, or social media know-how were being left behind when medical disasters struck.

When you call 02 8351 5854, you talk to a real volunteer. We write your story, verify hospital and barangay records, create your private donation page, and connect multi-rail payments — PayPal, GCash, Maya, and bank transfer. We collect the money for you, then process and remit it to you directly.

Our privacy guarantee

Only campaign creators share their links.

We never turn people's vulnerability into a public spectacle.

Other crowdfunding platforms put patients and calamity victims into public, searchable results where neighbours, debt collectors, or scammers can exploit them. At I Fund Pinoy Life, we refuse to do that.

Each campaign is 100% private and unlisted. Only the family running the campaign receives their private link to share with their trusted community, relatives, and church members. Donors who wish to assist verified causes can join our weekly private email list.

Honest & open

Why we take 10% + 3% on withdrawals

10%

Volunteer allowances & operations

Modest allowances for coordinators who answer calls six days a week, telephone landline bills, verification logistics, and server costs. The 10% keeps our volunteers answering families in need.

3%

Payment gateway processing

PayPal, Visa/Mastercard networks, and banking integrations deduct standard merchant fees. The 3% covers these third-party costs.
